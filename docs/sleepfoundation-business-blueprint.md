## 1. Executive Snapshot (<=400 words)

[VERIFIED] SleepFoundation.org presents itself as a trusted sleep advocate and organizes the site around Sleep Products, Sleep Solutions, Sleep Health, and About/Trust navigation. [VERIFIED] The homepage routes visitors into mattress, pillow, home sleep test, CPAP, sleep aid, snoring, sleep apnea, insomnia, baby sleep, quiz, and newsletter journeys. [VERIFIED] The site visibly mixes educational health content, commercial product recommendations, product cards with shop buttons, testing methodology, editorial policy, advertising disclosure, medical advisory board, and a medical disclaimer.

[HIGH-CONFIDENCE INFERENCE] The business model is a trust-led content-commerce funnel: broad sleep-health education builds topical authority and trust; commercial best-of, review, comparison, CPAP, sleep-test, supplement, bedding, and tool pages convert high-intent users; trust pages reduce YMYL and affiliate skepticism. [HIGH-CONFIDENCE INFERENCE] Mattresses, pillows/bedding, CPAP/sleep apnea services, anti-snoring products, supplements, and sleep tech are the primary monetizable verticals. [HYPOTHESIS] Revenue is a mix of affiliate commission, cost-per-click, direct referral/lead-gen, owned-network referrals, newsletter value, and possible sponsored placements explicitly labeled as such.

For Miran San, the strategy is not to copy expression but to reproduce the operating system: Serbian IA, hub/spoke SEO, original page templates, disclosed affiliate/direct-deal layer, strict medical review gates, local buying data, and benchmark parity scoring. The Serbian opportunity is strongest where global sites are weak: domestic mattress/pillow retailers, local delivery/returns/installment policy, Serbian-language medical explainers, apartment noise, summer heat, shift work, stress/burnout, and realistic budget tiers.

Immediate build priority: preserve the trust stack before monetization. The MVP should include core hubs, 20 P0 pages, templates for all major page types, legal/compliance pages, source/review workflows, and a parity loop that keeps functional similarity high while minimizing plagiarism and medical-claim risk.
