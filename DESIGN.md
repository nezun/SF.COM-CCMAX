# Design Notes

## Product principle

Miran San should become a Serbian sleep authority plus conversion infrastructure, not a thin affiliate blog. The model is editorial trust first, monetization second, and local defensibility always.

## Benchmark loop

1. Collect or seed SleepFoundation URL/navigation evidence.
2. Classify pages by type, funnel intent, category, and trust/commercial role.
3. Generate a source-of-truth JSON and readable HTML report.
4. Compare Miran San's planned IA/templates/trust/commercial layers to the benchmark.
5. Produce a parity score and a prioritized gap list.

## Local moat

SleepFoundation can be functionally benchmarked, but Miran San should outperform locally through Serbian language, Serbian expert layer, domestic retailer/brand data, local delivery/returns/installment policies, climate/noise/work-pattern guides, and region-specific compliance.
